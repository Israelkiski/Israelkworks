About Israel Kim

Music

Israel Kim is a high school composer, best known for his work "Ombra e Luce", a 4 movement symphony performed by the Phoenix Symphony. He has written many solo works for piano and cello, as well as chamber works like string quartets and brass quintets. Israel has attended camps around the country from New York and Michigan, as well as ones in his home state of Arizona.

Photography

Israel Kim is a photographer with experience in headshots, event photography, photoshoots, etc. He has been a photographer for SOUNDS Academy for 2 years, as well as traveling across the country while taking professional headshots and offering photoshoots.
